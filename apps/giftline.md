## Set it once and leave it

Pick the cart total, pick the product, write the line the shopper sees. That is the whole setup.

<figure markdown="1">
![Giftline's settings card in the Shopify admin: a "Give a free gift" toggle turned on, a cart total to qualify set to $50.00, the chosen gift product Alpine Trail Wax, and the buyer-facing text "Free gift with your order".]({{ '/assets/img/settings.png' | relative_url }}){: width="989" height="439" }

Giftline draws nothing. The gift is a normal cart line in your own theme's markup, so there's
nothing to edit going in and nothing to clean up if you uninstall it.

## Why the take-it-back-out half matters

Shopify can already discount a gift to zero on its own. What it cannot do is put the gift in the
cart, or take it out again.

So a shopper adds the gift, then removes something else, and the cart drops under your threshold.
The discount stops applying. The gift stays where it is, now at full price, and they check out
paying for the thing you advertised as free. It happens silently, and from their side of the screen
it looks like a trick.

So Giftline pulls the line the second the cart stops qualifying. If the discount ever breaks, it
pulls the line rather than let you charge for it.

## What it won't do

No tiers. No gift picker. No BOGO. No progress bar in the cart.

If you want those, buy BOGOS or Monk — they're bigger, they cost more, and they do all of it.
Giftline won't grow into that. It does one offer shape, on purpose, and that's why it's one screen
and why there's nothing of ours in your theme.
