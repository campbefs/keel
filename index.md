## What it does

- **Set any cart total.** $50, $250, whatever the offer is.
- **Counted after discounts.** A $60 cart with 20% off is a $48 cart, so it doesn't qualify for a
  $50 threshold. The gift itself never counts toward the total.
- **The gift removes itself.** Cart drops under your threshold and the line comes out, so nobody
  checks out paying for something you advertised as free.
- **Shoppers can decline it.** If they delete the gift, it stays gone. Giftline doesn't put it back.
- **Write the line they see.** "Free gift with your order", or anything else up to 120 characters.
- **Your other discounts keep working.** Shopify won't combine most discounts unless you tick a box
  on each one. Every other app in this category tells you to go and do that. Giftline does it for
  you, on every save.
- **Nothing goes into your theme.** The gift is a normal cart line in your own markup. No snippets,
  nothing to clean up if you uninstall.
- **Cart page and drawer**, in whatever currency the shopper is buying in.
