## Email

**[campbefs@gmail.com](mailto:campbefs@gmail.com)**

Same address for Giftline support and for custom app questions. No form, no ticket number, no
autoresponder.

## Want something built?

There's no form. Four things get you a useful reply instead of three rounds of questions:

1. **What the store sells**, roughly, and how big it is.
2. **What you're doing now.** The spreadsheet, the manual step, the workaround.
3. **What you want instead**, described the way you'd explain it to a new hire.
4. **Whether it has to talk to anything outside Shopify.** An ERP, a 3PL, a supplier feed.

I'll tell you if it isn't worth building. That's the answer more often than not, usually because
Shopify already does it or a $10 app already does.

## Something broken in Giftline?

Email works. So does the
[public issue tracker](https://github.com/campbefs/giftline-support/issues), if you'd rather write
it up where other merchants can see it. Every defect goes there with what caused it, including
[the one Shopify's reviewers found](https://github.com/campbefs/giftline-support/issues/1) before
the app was approved.
